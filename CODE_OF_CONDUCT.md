# Code of conduct

ixnos-data adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for every space the project uses: issues, pull requests, discussions
and any chat or event run in its name.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and remember
that people take part in Greek, English or both. Harassment, personal attacks and
publishing others' private information are not tolerated. That includes information about
people who appear in the public records ixnos-data shows.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@vwdshka](https://github.com/vwdshka),
privately. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer will act according to the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
